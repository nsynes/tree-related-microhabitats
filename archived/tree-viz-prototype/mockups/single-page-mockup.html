Description

Rot holes are cavities in the trunk or branches caused by internal decay of heartwood. They typically form where branches have broken off or where wounds have allowed fungi to enter the tree. These features are extremely valuable for wildlife, providing secure nesting and roosting sites.

🔍 Identification Tips
  • Look for dark openings in trunk or major branches
  • May have irregular edges with exposed decayed wood
  • Often shows evidence of use (droppings, nesting material)
  • Interior usually dark brown to black from decay
